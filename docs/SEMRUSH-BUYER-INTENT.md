# Semrush — buyer-intent keywords only

Country: **United States**
Tool: **Keyword Magic Tool**

## Filters to set before exporting

- **Intent: Commercial + Transactional** (untick Informational and Navigational)
- **KD: under 40**
- **Volume: 20+**  (buyer keywords are small — do not filter these out)
- **Exclude keywords containing:** rei, near me, walmart, amazon, ozark, koa

Export each group as CSV. Columns: Keyword, Volume, KD %, Intent, CPC.

## What to send back

The CSVs, plus one thing Semrush will not tell you: for the best three or four
keywords, actually run the search on Google and look at page one. If every
result is Etsy, Amazon or Pinterest, a standalone site will struggle there no
matter what the KD says. That check takes a minute per keyword and is worth
more than any metric in the export.

## Separately — the affiliate research

This is likely the larger earner, because it monetises the 40 articles that
already exist without the visitor having to buy anything of ours.

There is nothing to look up in Semrush for this. What is needed instead:

1. Whether Payoneer will open an account for you, which is the usual route to
   getting paid by US affiliate programmes.
2. Which of these programmes accept an affiliate based in Israel — this could
   not be verified from here and has to be checked by applying:
   Amazon Associates, AvantLink, Awin, REI, Backcountry.

Once one of them accepts you, the articles that already discuss jackets, base
layers, water filters, fire starters and packs get contextual links, and each
one becomes a second income stream on the same traffic.
