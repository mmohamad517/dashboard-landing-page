### Why they're sold together

Packing well is what stops most problems from starting. Knowing what to do is what stops the
rest from getting worse. A perfect packing list does nothing for you at the moment something
goes wrong, and a survival deck doesn't stop you arriving cold and wet in the first place.

---

### Who it's for

Beginners and families heading somewhere fairly normal — a campsite, a marked trail, a weekend
in the hills. Scout groups and youth leaders who want something to do round a fire that isn't
a lecture. If you already teach this for a living, you don't need it.

### What it isn't

It is a learning aid, not a course. It is **not** a first aid course and not a substitute for
one — the emergency cards are memory prompts for people who have already been taught this
material.

It deliberately does **not** teach wild plant or mushroom identification. Deadly and edible
species look alike, field tests don't reliably work, and a healthy adult can go a long time
without food. One card tells you to leave it alone, and explains why.

Several cards involve flame. Fire rules vary by region and season — check before you light
anything.
