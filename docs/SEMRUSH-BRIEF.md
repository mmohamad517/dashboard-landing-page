# Semrush — what to pull, and which columns

Country: **United States** (biggest slice of the English-speaking market).
Semrush is really a desktop tool — do this on a computer if you can.

The column that matters most is **KD % (Keyword Difficulty)**. The last export
had Volume and CPC but no difficulty, and difficulty is what decides whether a
brand-new site can rank at all. Please make sure it is included.

Export every table as CSV and send them over.

## Columns needed on every export

- Keyword
- Volume
- **KD %**
- Intent  (Semrush labels these Informational / Commercial / Transactional / Navigational)
- CPC
- Results  (number of competing pages, if the export offers it)

## What NOT to bother pulling

Skip anything in these groups. They looked attractive in the last export and
none of them are worth an article on this site:

- Head terms: "backpacking", "hiking" on its own. Ambiguous intent — half the
  people searching "backpacking" mean cheap travel, not walking — and locked up
  by REI, Wikipedia and AllTrails.
- Anything with "near me", a place name, or a named trail. Local or
  navigational intent; nobody searching it wants a layering guide.
- Physical gear terms: boots, poles, packs, sandals, cameras. Retailer
  territory, and we do not sell gear.
